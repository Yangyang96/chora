# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our standard

Participation in Chora should be safe, focused, and respectful. Be considerate
of different experience levels and backgrounds. Discuss ideas and evidence,
not personal traits. Give actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, and
correct mistakes without harassment or humiliation.

Unacceptable behavior includes threats, harassment, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, exposing private
information, or publishing credentials and security reports.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project
elsewhere. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or reject work
that violates it, using a response proportionate to the behavior and the safety
of affected people.

The project does not currently provide a private conduct-reporting channel.
Do not put sensitive reports or personal information in public project spaces.
Public issues and pull requests may still be moderated, but they are not a safe
substitute for private reporting. This limitation is intentional for the first
source publication and will be revisited before broader community participation.
No response time or appeal process is promised.
